How a respray actually happens

Four stages, not a rush job

Skipping a stage is how a respray looks fine for a month and wrong in the sun a year later. We don't skip stages.

01

Prep

Panel sanded, masked and primed properly before any colour goes near it.

02

Match

Colour matched to your vehicle's actual factory code, not eyeballed.

03

Spray

Base coat, then clear coat, applied for an even, factory-level finish.

04

Cure & check

Booth-cured and inspected in daylight before it's called done.
